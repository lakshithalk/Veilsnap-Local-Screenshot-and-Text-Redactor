# Code of Conduct

VeilSnap is intended to be a calm, constructive project for practical privacy tooling. Participants must treat each other with respect, focus criticism on work rather than people, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or publishing another person’s private information.

Do not post credentials, personal screenshots, customer information, or private logs in public project spaces. Report conduct concerns or sensitive material privately to the maintainer.

Project maintainers may remove contributions, comments, or issues that create risk for users or do not meet this standard.
